Platform and interaction plan

Chosen tool: a self-contained HTML page with CSS and vanilla JavaScript. It opens in any modern browser and does not depend on Canva, a login, or an internet connection for the interaction itself.

  • Every milestone is a keyboard-accessible button.
  • Clicking opens a detail panel with What happened, Why it matters, Subfield, Impact, and source links.
  • Search and layer filters help users find events.
  • The research register provides a compact scan of all 26 milestones.
  • The Print / Save as PDF control supports a static handout.
Historical accuracy note: The 1955 Dartmouth proposal named the field; the 1956 workshop helped establish it. The 1986 backpropagation paper was an influential demonstration and popularisation, not the first discovery of the algorithm. ImageNet was introduced in 2009 and its large-scale challenge began in 2010. The AI winters are shown as two commonly cited periods, not as years when all AI research stopped.
